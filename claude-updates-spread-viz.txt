What was wrong: tab-bar received the active-tab atom as a prop, then dereferenced it inside a lazy for. Reagent only tracks atom derefs that happen eagerly during a component's render execution — the lazy sequence from for can be realized outside that tracking window, so Reagent never knew tab-bar needed to re-render.

Fix: tab-bar now takes current-tab (the plain keyword value, e.g. :viz) and an on-change callback. The parent passes @active-tab and a reset fn. Since Reagent sees a changed keyword prop on click, it always re-renders tab-bar with the new active value, and mapv replaces for to eliminate the laziness issue entirely.
